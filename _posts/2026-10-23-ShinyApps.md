# Shiny Apps 
## Expert Elicitation Tool
https://github.com/KarlLamothe/Expert-Elicitation-ShinyApp

An R Shiny application developed to support structured expert elicitation for ecological decision-making under uncertainty. The application provides tools for summarizing, aggregating, and visualizing expert judgements expressed as probabilistic estimates.

## SAR Freshwater Fish Species Explorer
https://github.com/KarlLamothe/DFO-SAR-Intelligence-Tool

An R Shiny application for exploring Canadian freshwater fish species listed under the <i>Species at Risk Act</i>. The application was developed to make information available through the Species at Risk Public Registry easier to access, explore, and summarize. The application brings together species profiles, threat information, species documents, recovery planning information, and species imagery in a single interface for Schedule 1 freshwater fishes listed under SARA.
