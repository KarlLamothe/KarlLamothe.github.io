## [Expert Elicitation Tool](https://github.com/KarlLamothe/Expert-Elicitation-ShinyApp)

<i>R Shiny | Decision Analysis | Scientific Facilitation</i>

An interactive Shiny application developed to support expert elicitation workshops and structured decision-making processes. The tool helps facilitators and participants efficiently summarize, aggregate, and visualize expert judgments, providing a transparent framework for capturing and comparing expert opinions.

Users can enter, review, and synthesize individual expert assessments, with built-in functionality to generate summary statistics and graphical outputs that highlight areas of agreement, uncertainty, and divergence among participants. Interactive visualizations allow expert opinions to be explored in real time, supporting productive discussion and informed consensus-building during meetings.

Designed for scientific advisory processes, risk assessments, and conservation planning exercises, the application streamlines analytical workflows and improves the communication of expert knowledge. By automating data aggregation and visualization, it reduces manual processing and enables participants to focus on interpretation and decision-making.

<b>Key Features</b>: Expert opinion aggregation • Real-time visualization • Summary statistics • Consensus support • Meeting facilitation tools

## [SAR Freshwater Fish Species Explorer](https://github.com/KarlLamothe/DFO-SAR-Intelligence-Tool)

<i>R Shiny | Conservation Science | Decision Support</i>

An interactive Shiny application developed to support the exploration of Canadian freshwater fish species listed under the <i>Species at Risk Act</i>. The tool brings together species profiles, recovery planning information, threat assessments, and supporting documentation into a single, user-friendly interface.

Users can search and filter species by SARA status, geographic range, species, or population/designatable unit, enabling efficient access to relevant conservation information. Interactive summaries and visualizations allow users to examine threat patterns, recovery planning coverage, and species-specific details, while integrated document access simplifies the review of recovery strategies, action plans, and related reports.

Designed for scientists, resource managers, and conservation practitioners, the application streamlines the process of locating, synthesizing, and exploring species-at-risk information, supporting evidence-based research, management, and recovery planning.

<b>Key Features</b>: Species profiles • Threat analysis • Recovery planning summaries • Geographic filtering • Document explorer
